Privacy policy

Arktic Bear Labs · Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information the Rise app handles and how. It describes how Rise works today, and we'll update it as the app changes.

Information stored on your device

To work, Rise stores the following on your iPhone:

  • Your answers to the onboarding quiz and the routine built from them
  • Your rituals: names, emoji, durations, the days they repeat, and any links you add
  • Which rituals you checked off or skipped, and when
  • Vacation days you've planned
  • Your notification settings

This information may be included in your device's backups, such as iCloud Backup if you've turned it on, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

The current version of Rise doesn't require an account and doesn't send your routine or activity to us.

Future versions may add features such as accounts and syncing across devices, and may collect usage and crash information to help us understand how Rise is used and fix problems. If they do, we'll update this policy to describe what's collected, why, and the choices you have, and the App Store listing will reflect it.

We don't sell your personal information.

Notifications

Rise schedules reminders on your iPhone. You can turn each kind on or off in Rise's Profile tab, or turn them all off in iOS Settings.

Links you add

If you attach a link to a ritual, Rise opens it in Safari or in the app it belongs to (for example, a fitness app). What happens after that is governed by that website's or app's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting Rise from your iPhone removes the information Rise stored on it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll post the new version here and change the effective date above.
