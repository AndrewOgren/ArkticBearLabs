Common questions

How do I skip a ritual?

On Today, tap the ⋮ on the ritual's card (or long-press the card) and choose Skip for today. Skipping moves you on to the next ritual and doesn't break your streak.

How do I take a day off or plan a vacation?

Tap the ⋯ next to the date on Today and choose Take today off or Plan a vacation…. You can also plan and cancel vacations from the Profile tab. Your streak pauses and reminders stop on those days.

How do I add a link to a ritual?

Edit the ritual and paste a link into the Link field. A button on the ritual's card opens it. Links to apps like Peloton open the app if it's installed.

I do a different class every day. Can I add a link for each day?

Yes. Edit the ritual and turn on New link each day. Then choose Add links… from the ⋯ menu on Today to add links for the week ahead. Rise reminds you the evening before if any are missing.

Can I change a ritual for just one day?

Yes. When you save an edit, Rise asks whether it applies to just that day or to every repeat.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on for Rise in iOS Settings → Notifications, and that the reminders you want are switched on in Rise's Profile tab. Reminders also pause on vacation days.

How does the streak work?

Check off at least one ritual a day to keep your streak going. Days with nothing scheduled, vacation days and rituals you skip don't count against you.

Is my data backed up or synced?

Your routine is stored on your iPhone and included in your device backups. Syncing between devices isn't available yet. See the privacy policy for details.
